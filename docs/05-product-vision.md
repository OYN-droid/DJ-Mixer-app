# Product vision

DeckForge is fun first and powerful over time. It should make users feel like DJs on day one, then let them grow into producers at their own pace.

Manual DJing is a first-class workflow. A beginner should be able to load two tracks and make a mix quickly. Advanced controls should appear through progressive disclosure, without blocking the core path.

AI is optional. It can inspire, explain, recommend and automate repetitive work. AI suggestions must never prevent manual control. Human creativity has the final say.

DeckForge celebrates DJ culture, mixtapes, hip hop, house music, sampling, remixing and live performance. It respects the craft while welcoming first-time DJs, beatmakers and producers.
