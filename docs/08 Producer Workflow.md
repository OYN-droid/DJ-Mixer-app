# Producer Workflow

The producer workflow turns a musical direction into connected, editable material.

## Establish the project

Set the project identity, tempo, key, references, mood, energy, and intended format. Project Intelligence gathers current evidence from DITC, decks, patterns, pads, stems, and arrangement without overriding manual choices.

## Build rhythm in Beat Forge

Start from finger drumming, a preset, a saved pattern, or a previewed prompt plan. Select a kit independently from groove. Develop multi-bar rhythm, velocity, timing, probability, and automation. Record or send the result to Arrangement or Pads.

## Compose in Harmony Lab

Play an instrument manually, build chords, generate bass movement, record a melody, or preview an AI Composer plan. Harmony Match may use the active deck and Beat Forge pocket to recommend compatible space, voicing, or register.

## Shape samples in Pads

Load one-shots, long samples, deck regions, stems, or Beat Forge renders. Chop, categorize, organize banks and scenes, record performances, and send useful clips forward.

## Separate and remix in Stem Lab

Create stems where source rights and capability allow. Audition, route, combine, and send stems into decks, pads, or Arrangement with clear provenance.

## Arrange and develop

Place patterns, performances, audio clips, transitions, and automation on the timeline. Preserve event timing, velocity, groove, kit, instrument, and source references.

## Refine in Producer Studio

Review project-aware suggestions, creative missions, structural gaps, and alternative directions. Preview and explain recommendations before applying them.

## Finish and preserve

Save milestones, resolve missing sources, review playback ownership, record or render the final result, and preserve enough project state to reopen and revise the work.
